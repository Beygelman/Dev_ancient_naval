# v020.6 — The Watchful Voyage

4 October 2026. Previous released archives and the user's primary Git checkout/index remain untouched.

New voyages remove combat-hull upkeep, add one Thor to settlement income, increase dock sight to two cells and Granado movement to three. Granado's mortar covers distances two through five; other mortar dead zones stay unchanged. Double charges use the same pre-volley damage, capped by remaining target HP. New optional rules retain historical defaults when old embedded snapshots are loaded.

Turn guidance counts each owned object once if a useful, affordable, capacity-legal order remains. It includes legal travel and attacks which survive the expected reply, combat production, city upgrades and active collection/dock/capture/relic sites. Walls, ports, radar, repair and Support Brig production do not qualify. Radar-only contacts use a conservative worst-case return-fire estimate without disclosing class or HP. The presentation retains this command-derived query across selection and camera changes. Hints toggle separately from the battle save; when enabled, mouse and Space ending the turn first stamp a parchment and show a five-column object confirmation. Modal input blocks map, repair and duplicate turn orders. Reward and pictorial action hints share the same preference and English/Ukrainian/Dutch catalog.

Construction drawings use white rough pencil lines and drafting notches. Lighthouse ghosts show observation-safe prospective connections within the same six-transition route limit. Hover changes no aggregate, RNG, terrain raster or wave geometry. Building a lighthouse or cannon tower over a treasury removes the functional site and its retained scenery without issuing loot; hidden observations keep their remembered state.

Object placement keeps the outermost ring empty, separates starting escorts from their flagship and makes pirate counts depend on Lake/Bay/Sea/Ocean (2/3/4/5). Resource spots are slightly more frequent. Town houses use one medium scale, with inland relocation and compact plans for narrow soil; names follow their actual placement. Rivers avoid inland beaches, narrow necks, towns and peaks, prefer cove outlets and limit terminal rivers. Estuary blending starts at the shore and preserves destination-water tint. Decorative rivers remain non-navigable illustrations.

Faction sanctuary effects run only on observed visible towns/hulls and retain static rasters: blue dome waves, purple silver glints, yellow pink light, red crystal motes, green leaves, white crimson R with moving highlights. More bounded gulls flee observable nearby gunfire with twice the flight speed and 1.5 wing rate. Retained sea strips gain shader relief/grain. Sinking reconstructs separate solid three-dimensional parts in the same isometric projection: keels, split terraces, houses, sanctuary base/crown/rubble, masts and cannon carriages. Each part rotates and falls independently; clipping its faces at physical sea height produces opaque immersion. There are no hull-image slices. Debris is released afterward; collapsed fleets still follow their flagship and staged impacts commit once.

Optional upper-left advice appears once per voyage for resources, the first own Kolonel, two connected owned port towns, a repairable ship and installed radar. Each closeable paper includes an actual native-rendered close-up from a controlled public game fixture. Advice is nonmodal, suspends during menus/commands/rewards, and keeps a small separate presentation history across Continue. Its captions and reward/capture/turn guidance sit below their parchment surfaces. Disabling hints hides advice and bypasses end-turn confirmation.

Validated rules, native input/scenery/animation and final renderer gates are documented in [Validation](diagnostics/v020.6/Validation.md). Native pacing uses Ocean/four rivals in Pangaea overview and live fog; limits are unchanged. Local measured results describe the tested host, not every machine or driver.
