# v020.5 — The Painted Voyage

3 October 2026. Previous releases remain immutable checkpoints.

The new-voyage menu is a worn rolling papyrus on the right. A centered logo leads into six fleet emblems, an independent Lake/Bay/Sea/Ocean area, one to four painted rival figures, three dry-brush seamanship signs and four illustrated world choices. Selected options reveal a burgundy outline; hovered choices remain faint. Mouse-wheel scrolling rolls the decorative lips without showing a scrollbar. Embark stamps a four-finger hand, waits half a second, folds the paper and fades to black. A smooth descent toward the player's flagship ends with the elders' faith-specific welcome and explicit Set sail acceptance. Generation runs off the UI thread; repeated submissions and map orders are locked throughout the ceremony. Continue resumes directly without replaying it.

Language and interface scale belong to Settings in both home and game menus. English, Ukrainian and Dutch messages cover the entire new flow. The setup's height remains at most 60% of the logical viewport at 80–125% interface scale. This scrollbar removal applies to voyage setup; the interface scale slider remains inside Settings.

Support Brig is the current name/model of serialized Fishing. It has workshop houses and a crane, with a shared workshop icon for production and amphora badges. It collects fish resources, places fishing docks, builds defensive cannon towers and lighthouses, and earns no passive currency. Tower construction keeps the level-2 flagship unlock and spends the builder's actions. Active repair restores three HP and commits the remaining action budget; ship and town passive healing retain four HP, ancient towers two. New paid town upgrades cost 5/7/10/15 Thors; docks provide two per turn. Flagship levels still pay two Thors once; the redundant reward caption is removed from upgrade choices.

Construction hover uses the same legal production cells as Core. The chosen cell shows a translucent volumetric blueprint; cannon towers show their firing contour, lighthouses their sight contour. Illegal, occupied, hidden and cancelled sites show no ghost. Repeated hover reuses retained drawing commands and cannot mutate resources, vision, terrain or simulation randomness.

Direct sea connections have a six-transition navigable-route limit. Shared route segments remain deduplicated; existing connected networks may still join through intermediary ports. Cosmetic three-skin merchants follow those actual sea lanes. They are not gameplay ships and do not consume fleet capacity or alter economy.

Towns use a consistent medium-scale envelope fitted to contiguous inland ground, with five initial homes flanking the sanctuary and seventeen at the highest level. Buildings grow taller, while ground/wheat/fortifications stay clipped off beaches, lakes and surrounding water. Port roads remain beige and below buildings. Scenic river bends vary within cells, avoid town and peak footprints, and join only at planned confluences. Rounded estuaries extend from actual inland water across wide beaches, then fade into existing coastal shallows. Overlapping convex segments prevent self-intersecting channels and sand repainting water. These rivers do not change navigation.

Observed uncollected sea ruins emit upward light without requiring an adjacent ship. After treasure collection the light fades, scenery stays, and the ruin has no further selection, information parchment or action. Fog still controls which ruins can be drawn.

Explicit map areas no longer depend on rival count. The smallest area still supports five complete starting fleets and the required separated coastal settlements. The generator retries a bounded deterministic landscape salt on the same mesh, preserving the chosen seed and area; sampled Lake/four-rival layouts preserve fifteen towns more than three cells apart. Saved MapSize is optional. Older saves retain their embedded balance, old Fishing construction restrictions, unlimited route policy and historical town passive-heal fallback. Start a new voyage for all revised rules.

Native timing gates use the largest Ocean area with four rivals, both full Pangaea overview and live-fog island exploration. Their limits remain unchanged. A passing local frame report is evidence for the tested host and cases, not a guarantee for every machine or driver.
