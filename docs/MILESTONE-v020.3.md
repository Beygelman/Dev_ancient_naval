# v020.3 — Rivers and Revelations

3 October 2026. Builds and saves from earlier releases remain separate.

New voyages use the revised rules in `data/balance.json`: fishing ships, docks and the Mothership gain one sight tile; the flagship's cannon reaches two tiles; veteran Galleons and Kolonels gain one cannon tile. Mortars fire at distances 3–5, including the ancient tower. Fishing ships appear as anonymous radar contacts; Brigs remain radar-stealthy.

Fleet capacity counts the Mothership, combat ships and fishing ships. A flagship supplies 4 slots plus 2 per additional level; each living owned city supplies 2 plus 1 per additional level. Balloons and structures consume no slots. Losing capacity prevents construction without destroying existing ships. Own non-flagship units can be dismantled without a refund. Fishing ships can build lighthouses; villages cannot. Active/passive repairs restore up to 4 HP; ancient towers passively restore up to 2. Promotion restores full health.

New cities have more than three cells of separation. Pirate bays range from level 1 to 4, with two guaranteed level-3 fortified bays. Neutral settlements and pirates keep their starting level until captured; nation-owned cities grow through their existing two-turn cycle. Cosmetic rivers, mountain height/snow variation and land/forest/mountain ruins are independently seeded and do not alter movement, terrain or combat. Sea treasure ruins retain their art after collection and flash/fade after their treasure is committed.

Admiral planning assembles a pooled attack and estimates next-turn gun availability, retaliation and exposed approaches. Exploration follows only optical flagship sightings and then unknown frontiers. Remembered targets survive Continue; hidden motion and God's eye do not update those records.

Commands use a thinner, worn papyrus band. Attacks highlight legal observed silhouettes; a finishing volley adds red rays. Radar contacts never expose identity, health or finishing-shot estimates. Resource actions use small contours. Terrain alone has no information scroll. Keyboard: arrows/WASD pan, Space ends the turn, R repairs the selected ship. Menus, reward modals, flight and upgrades block gameplay shortcuts.

Nation discovery and fifth-turn religious offerings create saved pending receipts. Their vertical parchment has an explicit claim button; only claiming awards the currency. Save/Continue preserves both pending and claimed state. Messages and descriptions support English, Ukrainian and Dutch.

For historical voyages, optional new-rule defaults retain their original saved balance. Choose **New game** to use v020.3 rules. The artifact folder and embedded Windows product version are v020.3 / 0.20.3.0.

Validation evidence is recorded in `docs/diagnostics/v020.3/Validation.md` and the native logs. Native frame-pacing gates are required before packaging; headless tests do not establish smoothness.
