# v020.8 — The Living Scrolls

4 October 2026. Completes the latest papyrus, command UI, ending and optional-pirate requests. Historical archives, the beta roadmap, primary Git HEAD/index and real save slots remain preserved.

Papyrus content keeps its scale and layout during reveal/fold. A clipped viewport follows moving rolls; roll thickness grows as the paper winds in. Shared fibers, wear and edges unify all scrolls. Welcome, nation discovery, blessings and outcomes add polished faction rods and hanging depth-shaded animated ornaments. Everyday settings/advice/actions keep simpler rolls. Ceremonies retain modal input, fixed accessible footers and exact-once rewards.

The ready-object parchment is narrow and vertically centered on the right. The larger tilted amphora has foreground/background handles, texture, a ground shadow and faction patterns. It sits behind the end-turn paper with its printed count above. Radial actions contain no invisible gap sectors; town upgrade remains on a real central action. Brush accents occupy the text background, and outcome labels use dark paper ink. Victory fireworks are larger/brighter; defeat fire is bounded and stops with the overlay.

The new-voyage pirate checkbox defaults on. Disabling it generates no pirate towns or patrols, preserves neutral settlement opportunities and resources, and survives Continue. Enabled numbers follow world area and difficulty. Combat ship statistics and income are unchanged by this setting. Older snapshots keep their saved composition and missing fields have compatible defaults.

The selected-object counsel replaces the old basic card and separate information command. It opens automatically at lower left, with faction-colored embossed wax, a tied cord and vertical faction margin ornament. Name and small level precede the description; compact details use two columns and a native wheel viewport. Health is omitted from the card. Observed enemies use their own faction; radar contacts retain anonymous neutral styling.

Final Core/native renderer and export evidence: [validation](diagnostics/v020.8/Validation.md). Frame measurements describe this host and renderer, rather than a guarantee for every machine.
